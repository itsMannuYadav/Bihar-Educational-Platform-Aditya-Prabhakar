Screen 01

Dashboard — the one search box

No dashboard widgets competing for attention. One search box, a mic for voice, and just enough pickers to confirm class/subject/chapter. The primary CTA is the only saturated-color element on the page.

Bottom nav collapses the spec's seven top-nav items to the four a teacher touches daily — Search, Library, My Resources, Settings — with Saved Lessons, Worksheets and Analytics reachable one tap deeper. Desktop keeps the full top nav.

Screen 02

Generating — resource by resource

No single blank spinner for the whole kit. Each card resolves on its own: a cache hit shows "Ready" almost instantly (a teacher elsewhere already taught this chapter), while a genuine cache miss shows a short skeleton shimmer scoped to just that one card.

The teacher can open any finished card immediately — they don't wait for the slowest resource (usually the PPT render) to read the lesson plan.
